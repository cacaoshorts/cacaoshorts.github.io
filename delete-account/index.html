Account Management

Delete your Cacao Shorts account

Account deletion is permanent. You can delete your account directly in the app or request deletion by email if you cannot access the app.

Cacao Shorts is intended only for persons 18 years of age or older.

Method 1 — Fastest

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Cacao Shorts Android app and sign in.
  2. Select Profile in the bottom navigation.
  3. Select Delete Account in the Danger zone.
  4. Review both confirmation steps and type DELETE when prompted.
  5. Select Delete permanently.

This is the fastest method because the signed-in session verifies the account and submits the deletion directly.

Method 2 — Outside the app

Request deletion by email

Email cacaoshorts26@gmail.com with the subject “Cacao Shorts Account Deletion Request.”

Please:

  • Preferably send the request from the email associated with your Cacao Shorts account.
  • Identify the email address associated with the account.
  • Clearly request permanent deletion of the Cacao Shorts account.
Email an account-deletion request

We may request reasonable information to verify that you control the account before processing an external deletion request.

Never send sensitive authentication information by email.

Cacao Shorts will not ask you to email your password, access token, refresh token, or verification code.

What deletion removes

Permanent account deletion removes:

  • Your Supabase Auth account
  • Your profile and user roles
  • Your episode viewing progress and completion state
  • Your My List favorites
  • Your Continue Watching and series watch state
  • Your private series ratings
  • Your account-linked push-device and notification registrations

Information that may remain

The current Cacao Shorts Flutter app does not write analytics events. Existing event records, if any, may be retained with the user identifier removed. Limited de-identified, operational, or security records may also remain where reasonably necessary for service operations, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or applicable legal requirements. Service-provider logs and backups may follow provider configuration and legal requirements.
