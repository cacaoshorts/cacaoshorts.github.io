Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Cacao Shorts Android application.

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Cacao Shorts service. References to “Cacao Shorts,” “we,” “us,” or “our” mean Cacao Shorts in connection with the service.

2. Age Requirement

Cacao Shorts is intended only for people who are 18 years of age or older. You must not create an account or use the service if you are under 18.

3. Information We Collect

Guest Mode

You can use Cacao Shorts without first creating a registered account. On first use, the app automatically creates a temporary Guest identity and authenticated session. That Guest identity has an internal Cacao Shorts user identifier. It is not intended to identify you by your real-world name, but it is still an identifier associated with your Guest activity.

Guest viewing progress, episode-completion and Continue Watching state, My List choices, and private series ratings may be stored on our servers under that temporary Guest identifier. Cacao Shorts does not enroll a Guest in account-linked push notifications in the current version.

A Guest may choose to create a new account while keeping eligible Guest data, or sign in to an existing account and request a transfer. In an authorized transfer, viewing and progress state may be reconciled, My List may be combined and deduplicated, and ratings may be transferred. Existing permanent-account information may take precedence when the same item has conflicting data. The temporary Guest identity may then be removed after a successful transfer.

Keep access to your Guest session if you want to keep your Guest data.

If you clear Cacao Shorts app storage, uninstall the app, lose the device or session, or otherwise lose access before creating or signing in to an account, Cacao Shorts may no longer be able to reconnect you to the previously stored Guest data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete server-side Guest data.

Account and authentication information

  • Your email address when you create an account, sign in, or request password recovery.
  • An internal user identifier associated with a Guest identity or permanent account.
  • If you choose a third-party sign-in method such as Google, Cacao Shorts and its authentication provider receive information needed for that authentication, including your email address, provider account identifier, and related authentication metadata. The current Google flow requests identity and email access; Cacao Shorts does not request the Google profile scope or copy a provider profile picture into your Cacao Shorts profile.
  • The username you independently choose during account creation. It is not automatically derived from a provider display name or email. It is stored without the “@” character, associated with your authenticated account and user identifier, and displayed in your Profile as “@username.”
  • Your optional selection of a predefined Cacao Shorts profile avatar. These avatars are bundled and predesigned by Cacao Shorts; we store only the selected avatar identifier associated with your account. You do not upload personal profile-photo bytes, and provider profile pictures are not copied or used as your Cacao Shorts avatar.
  • Authentication and session information needed to keep you signed in and secure your account.
  • Account-related timestamps and authentication status maintained by our authentication provider.

Viewing and preference information

  • Episode viewing position, completion status, and playback timestamps.
  • Continue Watching information, including your last episode and position.
  • Series you add to My List.
  • Your choice to dismiss a series from Continue Watching.
  • Private 1–5 star series ratings that you voluntarily submit while signed in. Ratings are associated with your account and are not public reviews.

Device-local information

The Guest registration reminder stores minimal information only on your device: a one-way fingerprint scoped to the Guest identity and the time a reminder was dismissed, so the app can apply its 14-day cooldown. The reminder does not introduce new server-side collection. The app also stores limited local state needed to resume an account upgrade or an authorized Guest-data transfer. A temporary transfer credential is kept in platform-protected app storage while a transfer is pending. Cacao Shorts does not store your plaintext password or authentication token in that reminder or transfer state.

Push notification information

When Cacao Shorts starts, Firebase services used for push-notification infrastructure may automatically generate and process a Firebase Installation ID or related technical registration information. This may occur before you enable New series notifications.

If you have a permanent account and voluntarily enable that setting, Cacao Shorts may associate and register the device for account-linked new-series notifications. We may then store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device/app-instance registration token, your device platform (such as Android), your enabled or disabled preference, registration, update, and last-seen timestamps, and the association between that registration and your Cacao Shorts account. The in-app setting controls this account-linked registration and delivery through the notification feature.

Search and network information

The app downloads the available published catalog and filters it on your device as you type. Cacao Shorts does not transmit or retain the words you enter in the Search field. Like most online services, our service provider may process ordinary request information for catalog and other network requests, such as IP address, request time, requested endpoint, and technical HTTP or client information, for service delivery, security, and operations.

4. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The current Cacao Shorts app does not intentionally collect:

  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Advertising IDs or hardware device identifiers
  • Photos from your device
  • User-uploaded videos
  • Microphone or audio recordings
  • Files or documents
  • Analytics or crash-report data
  • Advertising information
  • Payment information

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticated sessions.
  • Identify your account within Cacao Shorts, display your chosen profile identity and predefined avatar, and support account functionality, profile customization, and account features.
  • Provide login, logout, and password-recovery features.
  • Stream episodes and provide access to private video content.
  • Save and restore viewing progress.
  • Provide Continue Watching and My List.
  • Provide the optional private series-rating feature.
  • Create and maintain Guest sessions, present a device-local registration reminder, and complete user-requested Guest account upgrades or data transfers.
  • Deliver requested new-series notifications, route a tapped notification to the relevant content, and maintain notification delivery functionality.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.
  • Protect the service, investigate misuse, and maintain reliable operations.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Authentication and Account Security

Passwords are entered in the app and transmitted to Supabase Auth for authentication. The Cacao Shorts Flutter app does not store plaintext passwords and cannot retrieve your password. Authentication sessions use access and refresh tokens. On Android, session information is stored in app-private storage so you can remain signed in on the same device.

Cacao Shorts supports sign-in through Google. Provider information is used for authentication, account creation, account linking, account security, and account management. Authenticated identities may be linked to the same Cacao Shorts account when Supabase and the provider determine that eligible verified account information matches; matching information does not guarantee that every identity will be linked automatically. Although support code for another provider may exist, Facebook sign-in is not offered in the current public interface. Cacao Shorts does not request friends, birthday, gender, location, posts, photo-library access, contacts, or advertising information through the currently offered login methods.

7. Viewing Activity and App Features

We associate viewing progress, completion status, Continue Watching, and My List with your account. This allows the app to resume episodes, select the next available episode, display completed episodes, and remember your saved series. Cacao Shorts does not currently use this information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

8. Service Providers

Cacao Shorts currently uses Supabase to provide authentication, database services, storage, signed private-video delivery, and Edge Functions. Supabase processes information on our behalf to provide these functions. Authentication emails may be delivered through infrastructure configured for the Supabase project.

If you choose social sign-in, the selected authentication provider processes authentication information under its own privacy terms. Cacao Shorts uses provider-supplied information made available through Supabase only for the authentication and account purposes described in this Policy.

Cacao Shorts uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google, for push notification infrastructure and to deliver notifications that users choose to enable. Firebase and Google may process technical registration information necessary to provide this infrastructure and notification delivery under their own privacy terms.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We disclose information to Supabase as needed to operate the service and to Firebase and Google as needed to provide push-notification infrastructure and deliver requested push notifications. When you choose social sign-in, relevant authentication information is also processed by that provider. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, protect users or the service, investigate fraud or abuse, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

10. Advertising

The current app contains no advertising SDKs, does not perform personalized advertising, does not share personal information for advertising, and does not track users across third-party apps or services for advertising purposes. Cacao Shorts uses push notification registration tokens for app notification functionality, not for advertising, cross-app tracking, or personalized ads.

11. Data Retention

Account and product data is generally retained while your account exists or until you remove the relevant item where the app supports removal, the account is deleted, or the information is no longer reasonably required to provide the service.

Private series ratings are retained while reasonably needed to provide the rating feature and maintain your account. They are deleted when you delete your account through the application's account-deletion flow.

Guest data is currently not subject to a fixed automatic deletion schedule. It may remain associated with the temporary Guest identifier while the Guest session exists and may be retained if that session is lost. If you create a new account using the Guest upgrade flow, eligible data remains with the same account. If you request a transfer to an existing account, eligible Guest data is reconciled with that account and the temporary Guest identity is removed after the transfer and related cleanup succeed. We may introduce a documented inactivity or cleanup process in the future.

The device-local Guest reminder and pending-flow information described above remains in the app's local storage until it is replaced or removed, including when app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. This device-local reminder information is not collected by Cacao Shorts on its servers.

Push notification registration data is retained while reasonably needed for notification functionality. When you disable New series notifications in the app, the device registration is disabled or removed according to the app's operation. You can re-enable the preference later. Account deletion removes notification registration data linked to the deleted account, subject to the provider-log and backup limitations described below. Disabling the in-app notification setting does not itself necessarily delete the Firebase Installation ID maintained by Firebase.

Limited operational or security records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or service operations, subject to applicable requirements. Service-provider logs and backups may follow the provider's configuration and applicable legal requirements.

12. Account and Data Deletion

If you have a permanent account, you can permanently delete it in the app by opening Profile and selecting Delete Account. You can also follow the instructions on our account-deletion page.

Account deletion removes the Supabase Auth user, profile (including the account-associated username), user roles, episode progress, favorites, series watch state, private series ratings, and account-linked push notification device registrations. The current app does not write analytics events. Limited legacy, de-identified, operational, security, or legally required records may remain as described in this Policy. We do not promise immediate deletion of provider operational logs or backups.

The current Guest Profile does not offer the permanent-account deletion control. If you still have access to an active Guest session and want help deleting its server-side data, contact us using the address below. We may need information available from that active session to verify and locate the Guest data safely. If the Guest session has already been lost, its pseudonymous nature may prevent us from reliably reconnecting a request to that data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not itself delete server-side Guest data.

Deleting your Cacao Shorts account does not delete an account held with a third-party sign-in provider and may not, by itself, revoke authorization previously granted to that provider. You can manage connected-app permissions separately through the provider's account settings. Provider-side records remain subject to the provider's own systems, retention practices, and privacy terms.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the current service, including authenticated access, database access controls, private video storage, temporary signed video URLs, and encrypted network transmission. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. International Data Processing

Cacao Shorts may be available to users in multiple countries. Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live, including locations where our service provider operates infrastructure. Those locations may have different data-protection laws.

Cacao Shorts is intended only for persons 18 years of age or older and is not directed to children. If you believe a person under 18 has provided account information, contact us so we can review the matter.
